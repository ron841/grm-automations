Addendum B · font load-bearing audit

JetBrains Mono confirmed load-bearing.

FamilyRoleUse countKeep?
NewsreaderDisplay / headlines / italic accents / dropcapall H1–H4, blockquotes, stats, review q'sKeep
Public SansBody, form inputs, names-in-bylinesall paragraph copyKeep
JetBrains MonoEyebrows, rail meta, nav, buttons, table headers, stat labels, slot captions, trust band, form labels, time stamps, footer headings17+ componentsKeep

The mono font is the "editorial chrome" — it's what makes the metadata rail, the eyebrows, and the data tables read as editorial product. Public Sans Medium was considered. It blurs the distinction between body and meta and flattens the three-voice typographic hierarchy to two. Declined.
