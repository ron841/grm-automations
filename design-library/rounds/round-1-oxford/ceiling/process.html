Step one

Strip the thatch. Reach the soil.

Step 1 · in progress photo · dethatcher pulling up thatch matgmb[3]

Thatch is the layer of dead grass stems and roots that builds up between green grass and the soil below. A little is fine. Too much, and water pools on top, fungus moves in, and anything we apply sits on the mat instead of reaching the actual soil.

We run a mechanical dethatcher across the whole lawn. The blades break the mat up and lift it. Then we short-cut what's left so the grass crown is exposed and the next step can reach what matters.

Step two

Pull plugs. Let the root zone breathe.

Step 2 · after aeration photo · aeration plugs across lawngmb[5]

Central Florida soils are sand-on-clay, and years of mowing compact the root zone into something roots cannot push through. Water beads up. Oxygen can't reach. The grass is growing on concrete.

The core aerator pulls thousands of cylindrical plugs of soil out of the lawn and drops them on the surface. The holes open channels for water, oxygen, and everything we are about to apply in step three.

Depth1 – 4 in
EquipmentWalk-behind or tow-behind core aerator
If skippedCompost sits on surface. Runs off in first rain. Money into the storm drain.
What you'll seePlugs everywhere. They break down naturally in a week.
Step three

Top-dress. Rebuild the living layer.

Step 3 · compost applied photo · ECOLAWN spreader applying compostgmb[1]

Life Soil's Comand is an organic compost engineered for lawn restoration. Nutrient-rich, full of soil biology, and sized to fall into aeration holes. We apply it liberally with an ECOLAWN spreader and rake it into the holes.

From underneath, the soil starts rebuilding its biology within days. Roots reach further. Water holds. The grass you already have starts growing like it finally can.

What you'll seeA light brown dusting across the lawn. Gone within days.
Before any of it

The free on-site assessment.

The assessment photo · Tracy walking a property with a clipboardclient

Before we schedule anything, Tom or Tracy comes out and walks the property. Grass type, irrigation, shade, soil feel, compaction. Maybe a soil plug to see what's under there.

Then we tell you what we think. Sometimes it's the full rehab. Sometimes it's one step. Sometimes it's "your lawn is fine, go mow it less aggressively." No charge either way.
