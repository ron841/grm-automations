Components · a la carte if needed

The three steps, individually. For the rare case.

Most lawns need all three. Occasionally a recently-rehabbed lawn only needs top-dressing. We'll tell you at the assessment.
01

Dethatching & Short Cutting

Strip the thatch mat blocking water, air, and treatments. Short cut so what comes next can reach the crown of the grass.

Step 1 · 0–1 in
Equipment: dethatcher
02

Core Aeration

Pull plugs of sand and clay from the compacted root zone. This is the step most lawn companies skip. It is the reason the compost works.

Step 2 · 1–4 in
Equipment: core aerator
03

Compost Top-Dressing

Life Soil's Comand organic compost, applied liberally and raked into the aeration holes. The soil biology rebuilds. The grass follows.

Product: Life Soil's Comand
