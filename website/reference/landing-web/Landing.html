— Section 02  ·  The diagnosis

Every Marion County contractor site we look at has the same three holes.

  1. 01
    No schema markup.
    The AI has to guess what trade you run, what you charge, where you work, and whether you are trustworthy. It usually guesses wrong or gives up.
  2. 02
    Marketing filler where the facts should be.
    Your homepage opens with "we pride ourselves on quality work" or similar. The AI needs your license number, years in business, service area, review count, and phone. Those are almost always missing from the first paragraph.
  3. 03
    AI crawlers blocked by accident.
    WordPress security plugins quietly return a 403 error to GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot. Your site is invisible to them not by choice. By default.

— Section 03  ·  The fix

What we ship on day one.

Every Get Rooted Media site ships with the AI citation playbook built in. You do not ask for it. You do not pay extra. It is what we build.

  1. 01
    Trade-specific Schema.org markup.
    Plumber, Electrician, RoofingContractor. Not a generic tag.
  2. 02
    FAQPage schema on every service page.
    The single strongest predictor of AI citation we have measured.
  3. 03
    Fact-dense first paragraph.
    License number, years in business, cities served, review count, phone. Every line is a citation anchor.
  4. 04
    Every AI crawler allowed.
    GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Bingbot, Googlebot. No plugin in the way.
  5. 05
    Images in WebP and AVIF.
    Your site loads in under two seconds on a phone. PageSpeed above 90.
  6. 06
    Quarterly content refresh.
    Every thirteen weeks we update your review counts, rotate a seasonal FAQ, and swap a project photo. AI assistants read that as freshness, and freshness earns citations.
