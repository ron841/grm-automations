## FOLLOWUP — Thursday

**Topic:** A simple three-touch follow-up system real estate agents can use in the 90 days after closing to generate referrals and repeat business.

**Hook:** The deal closed — and that's exactly when most agents disappear. Don't be that agent.

**Caption:**
The 90 days after closing are arguably the most valuable window you have to turn a one-time client into a lifelong referral source, and it costs nothing but a little intention and consistency. Here's a framework we love: Touch 1 — a handwritten card or small local gift (think something from a Marion County small business) within the first week after closing. Touch 2 — a personal check-in call or text at the 30-day mark asking how they're settling in. Touch 3 — a value-add message at 90 days, like a neighborhood market update or a local resource they'd genuinely find useful. Save this post, put it in your CRM workflow, and let your past clients feel the difference — referrals will follow.
