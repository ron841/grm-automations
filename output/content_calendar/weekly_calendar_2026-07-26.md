## FOLLOWUP — Thursday

**Topic:** A three-touch follow-up system real estate agents can implement the week after closing to turn a transaction into a long-term referral relationship.

**Hook:** The deal closed. Most agents disappear. The great ones are just getting started.

**Caption:**
The week after closing is one of the most underused opportunities in a real estate agent's business — your clients are emotional, grateful, and talking about their new home to everyone they know. A handwritten card on Day 1, a personal check-in call on Day 7, and a local business recommendation (think a great Ocala restaurant or a trusted AC company near them) on Day 14 turns a transaction into a relationship that generates referrals for years. It costs almost nothing and it's the kind of follow-up that makes clients say your name before anyone even asks for an agent recommendation.

**Hashtags:** #ClientFollowUp #RealEstateRelationships #OcalaRealtor #ReferralMarketing #AgentPlaybook

**Best Time to Post:** Thursday at 9:00 AM ET — Thursday mornings are when agents are wrapping up the week's business and thinking ahead. Follow-up and relationship content resonates strongly as they review their pipelines before the weekend.

**Hook:** You just bought your first home in Marion County. Florida has a few things it forgot to warn you about.
